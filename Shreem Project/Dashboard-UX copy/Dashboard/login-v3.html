Privacy Policy & Terms of Service
Effective March 1, 2026 · Updated March 16, 2026

1. Who We Are

RevGuard is a hospitality intelligence platform operated by Shreem Technologies Inc., providing multi-property hotel management software including revenue dashboards, task engines, and operational analytics.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account data: name, email, job title, property affiliation
  • Usage data: features used, session duration, browser type
  • Operational data: PMS exports, revenue figures, task records
  • Device data: IP address, device type, OS (security logging only)

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the RevGuard platform
  • To authenticate identity and prevent unauthorised access
  • To generate AI-powered insights and proactive alerts
  • To send security alerts and support communications

4. Google & Microsoft SSO

When using SSO, we receive your email, display name, and profile picture from the provider. We never store your SSO passwords. You may revoke access via your Google or Microsoft account settings at any time.

5. Security

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). Passwords are hashed using bcrypt (cost ≥ 12). We undergo annual SOC 2 Type II audits.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We may share with service providers under strict agreements, or when required by law.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data. Contact privacy@revguard.io for any requests. EU/UK users may also contact their local DPA.

8. Cookies

We use strictly necessary session cookies for authentication and optional analytics cookies. Manage preferences in account settings.
