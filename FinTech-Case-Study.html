Project Concept

The Business Problem

Fragmented approval chains forced finance teams through 6 manual steps with no system handoff between PR, PO and Invoice stages. 75% of users routinely lost track of invoices mid-flow, reverting to calls and emails to chase status updates. Budget decisions were flying blind — 60% of admins operated on stale Excel data, not live figures. The mandate: architect a validated, role-aware workflow that gives every stakeholder exactly what they need — and nothing they don’t.

✗

Phase 02 — Ideate

Workflow Architecture

Current-state friction mapped in Miro — redesigned to cut steps & add validation-driven automation.

Key Insights From Research
01
No Document Tracker

No way to track a specific document in case of urgency — teams had zero visibility into where a PR or invoice was stuck in the pipeline.

02
Silent Bottlenecks

No notification when a document stalled — the correct user was never alerted, causing invisible delays that only surfaced via calls and emails.

03
Missing Pre-Approval Layer

Finance teams lacked a pre-approval tracking system to verify documents before sending to vendor — leading to costly rejections post-dispatch.

04
The Design Mandate

Improved visibility, reduced workflow complexity, and proactive in-system tracking — eliminating every instance of out-of-system communication.

Visual Design System

Color Strategy

Every color decision was made for a specific data role — not decoration.

Authority & Trust

Deep blue (#2B4C8C) signals system authority — in financial interfaces, users interpret interface color as platform credibility. Blue was chosen for primary navigation, headers and interactive states to establish unwavering trust.

Alerts & Pending States

Ledger gold (#E8B23A) is reserved exclusively for warnings — maintaining WCAG AA contrast ratios across both light panels and dark modal overlays. It draws the eye without triggering false urgency.

Semantic Data Truth

Green and red are semantically locked to Approved / Rejected — never used decoratively. In dense invoice tables, overloading status colors with decorative use is a fast path to user error and mis-approval.

Scan Speed

Manrope 700–800 anchors dashboard headings — geometric, tight letterforms reduce horizontal eye travel in data-dense environments where users scan rows, not paragraphs.

Fatigue Prevention

Body at 16px, weight 400, line-height 1.65 — calibrated for long approval queues and invoice tables where tight leading causes scan fatigue in under 3 minutes.

Data Alignment

IBM Plex Mono reserved for numeric data & status codes — fixed-width columns prevent optical jitter when aligning financial figures and invoice IDs across table rows.
